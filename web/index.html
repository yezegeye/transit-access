How the numbers were made. A transit router written in C++ computed door-to-door travel times from each tract's population center to every Cook County tract, leaving every minute from 7 to 9 AM, and kept the median trip. Walks: up to 800 m to and from stops and 400 m between them, at 5 km/h along streets. Jobs reachable counts every job in Cook County tracts within the limit.

What it leaves out. CTA buses and trains only, no Metra or Pace. Scheduled service only, with no delays. Walking distances are straight lines with an average detour. Reachable jobs measure opportunity, not who works where. The travel-time map uses 2-minute transfers.

Data. Chicago Transit Authority GTFS schedules; U.S. Census Bureau ACS 2020–2024 and LEHD LODES 2024 jobs; 2020 tract boundaries; City of Chicago community areas. Code and method: github.com/yezegeye/transit-access.
